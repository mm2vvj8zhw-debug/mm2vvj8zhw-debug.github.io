The Value
of Zero

Little Zero doesn’t like school anymore. The other numbers are making fun of him. They say that since he is a zero, he has no value.

Join Little Zero on a day that will change his life—and help him discover his real value. A gentle story for young readers about kindness, confidence, and finding where you belong.

Meet the author

Hello, I’m
Robert.

Robert Vigil was born and raised in Colorado. After high school, he joined the United States Air Force and served as a Security Policeman.

His path has taken him to Kansas, Ohio, Florida—and even Walt Disney World. Through it all, he has always loved to read and write. Today, Robert lives in North Dakota with his husband and two cats. The Value of Zero is his first book.
